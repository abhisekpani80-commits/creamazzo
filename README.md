> *"The Pinnacle of Coffee Pleasure"*

## Key Features

- **Hero Showcase & Story:** Full-bleed interior & storefront photography with animated gold editorial typography and rotating `4.9 ★` Google Rating crest.
- **Interactive Menu:** Category filters (`Signature Favorites`, `Hot Coffee`, `Cold & Coolers`, `Bites, Pasta & Pizza`) featuring house pricing in INR (`₹`).
- **Verified Google Maps Reviews:** Interactive testimonial slider showcasing authentic guest reviews from Peerzadiguda & Uppal Bhagayath.
- **Direct WhatsApp Table Booking:** Single-step reservation form that pre-fills guest name, phone number, party size, date, time, and seating preference and redirects directly to Creamazzo's WhatsApp (`wa.me/919704030636`).
- **Curated Gallery & Map:** Lightbox photo gallery and interactive Google Maps embed for turn-by-turn navigation.
